About me

Design with a clear idea.

I’m a Digital Media Designer with a multidisciplinary creative background in graphic design, visual communication, branding, illustration, motion graphics, video editing, 3D modeling, and animation. My diverse academic and practical experience has equipped me with the ability to transform ideas into impactful visual experiences across different digital platforms.

Passionate about creativity, storytelling, and visual exploration, I combine artistic vision with technical skills to develop meaningful, engaging, and visually compelling work. I’m always eager to explore new techniques, challenge creative boundaries, and bring ideas to life through design and digital media.
